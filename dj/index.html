01 / How the set moves

Start warm.
Build the room.
Land together.

I plan the arc, mark the moments, and leave enough space to follow the crowd. Every set starts with a direction—not a fixed script.

  1. 01 Open

    Melodic house, disco, and easy grooves that let the room arrive.

  2. 02 Lift

    Pop, Bollywood, hip-hop, and familiar records with somewhere to go.

  3. 03 Peak

    Club edits, house, and throwbacks chosen for the moment—not the genre label.
