<h2>Research</h2>
<div class="p">
        I work on computer vision and medical AI—especially robustness, bias mitigation, simulation-driven augmentation, and segmentation.
        See the full list on the <a href="research.html">Research page</a>.
      </div>
<div class="cards">
<div class="card">
<div class="card-title">Physiologically-informed augmentation</div>
<div class="card-desc">Finite-element lung motion simulation to generate breathing-cycle variation and improve tumor segmentation performance.</div>
</div>
<div class="card">
<div class="card-title">Bias &amp; confounder mitigation</div>
<div class="card-desc">Methods to reduce spurious correlations in skin-lesion segmentation and image diagnosis, with large-scale evaluation.</div>
</div>
<div class="card">
<div class="card-title">Digital Twin direction</div>
<div class="card-desc">Modeling and learning pipelines that connect mechanistic structure with deep learning for clinically relevant imaging tasks.</div>
