<section id="research">
  <h2>Research</h2>
  <div class="p">
    I’m interested in computer vision for medical imaging and scientific applications. A lot of my current work focuses on
    reducing spurious correlations and improving generalization with principled augmentation (including physics/physiology‑informed approaches).
  </div>

  <div class="pub">
    <div class="pub-title">Enhancing Medical AI with Physiologically‑Informed Data Augmentation</div>
    <div class="pub-authors">Christoph Sadée, Alex Dils, Cally Lin, Audrey Chun, Francisco Carrillo‑Perez, Preya Shah, et al.</div>
    <div class="pub-venue">ICML 2026 submission (pending) — expected Jan 2026</div>
    <div class="pub-links">
      <a href="#" data-placeholder="true">Paper</a> / <a href="#" data-placeholder="true">Code</a>
    </div>
    <div class="pub-desc">

    </div>
  </div>
