Computer Science @ UC Berkeley · San Carlos / Berkeley, CA

Hi — I’m Alex. I work on computer vision and machine learning systems that need to hold up outside clean benchmarks: noisy data, messy real-world constraints, and (sometimes) high stakes.

I’ve spent the last several years doing applied research with Stanford Medicine, building and refining deep learning models for medical imaging and visual reasoning. Alongside research, I like shipping full-stack ML—right now I’m CTO at Appraise AI, where we’re turning real market data into consistent, photo-driven valuations.

If you’re here for the short version: I’m interested in how we make models reliable, not just accurate—robustness, calibration, and the small engineering decisions that decide whether something works in practice.

Research

My north star is making learning systems that behave well when the world doesn’t cooperate. I like problems where you have to combine modeling choices with careful evaluation and practical engineering.

Medical computer vision

Stanford Medicine · dermatology / imaging

I work on deep learning models for cancerous skin lesion detection, iterating on data pipelines, model training, and feedback-driven refinements. I care a lot about robustness and how models fail—because that’s where the learning is.

Generative vision models

stereo synthesis · hybrid analytical + deep learning

I’ve explored stereoscopic view synthesis from a single image using a mix of GAN-based approaches and depth-map geometry, then combined them into a composite method to improve perceptual quality.

Environmental & scientific ML

segmentation · microscopy · generalization

For microplastics analysis, I helped build segmentation models that use GAN-generated ecological context to expand training diversity. The goal: better performance when the “real world” looks different from the training set.

Systems that ship

deployment · retrieval · evaluation

Outside the lab, I enjoy building systems end-to-end: data → models → APIs → monitoring. It keeps me honest about what matters when people actually use the output.

Publications

AI-Driven Segmentation for Microplastic Analysis in Water Using GAN-Generated Ecological Context

Alex Dils, et al. · arXiv (Oct 2024)

We augment limited labeled microplastics imagery with GAN-generated ecological context and train a segmentation model that improves F1 performance versus training on real-only data.

Eye For An Eye: A Deep-Learning and Analytical Method to Spatializing Stereoscopic Images

Alex Dils · National High School Journal of Science (Apr 2025)

A study of three approaches to stereoscopic view synthesis—Pix2Pix, analytical depth methods, and a composite hybrid—showing the best results come from combining the strengths of both.
