Research

I’m broadly interested in computer vision and machine learning systems that operate under real-world constraints. My work spans medical imaging, environmental ML, and generative models.

Medical Computer Vision

At Stanford Medicine, I develop deep learning models for cancerous skin lesion detection, with a focus on robustness, data quality, and clinician-in-the-loop feedback.

Generative Vision Models

I study generative approaches to visual transformation and representation learning, including hybrid analytical–deep learning methods for stereoscopic image synthesis.

Environmental & Scientific ML

I’ve worked on segmentation models for microplastic detection in water samples, using GAN-generated ecological context to improve generalization on limited real-world data.
