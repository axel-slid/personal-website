Research

I work on computer vision and medical AI—especially robustness, bias mitigation, simulation-driven augmentation, and segmentation. See the full list on the Research page.
Physiologically-informed augmentation
Finite-element lung motion simulation to generate breathing-cycle variation and improve tumor segmentation performance.
Bias & confounder mitigation
Methods to reduce spurious correlations in skin-lesion segmentation and image diagnosis, with large-scale evaluation.
Digital Twin direction
Modeling and learning pipelines that connect mechanistic structure with deep learning for clinically relevant imaging tasks.
