Alex Dils

Computer Science @ UC Berkeley · Berkeley, CA

Hi — I’m Alex. I work on computer vision and machine learning systems that need to hold up outside clean benchmarks: noisy data, messy real-world constraints, and (sometimes) high stakes.

I’ve spent the last several years doing applied research with Stanford Medicine, building and refining deep learning models for medical imaging and visual reasoning. Alongside research, I like shipping full-stack ML—right now I’m CTO & co-founder at Appraise AI.

Broadly, I’m interested in robustness, bias/confounders, and evaluation that matches the real world—not just the leaderboard.

Research

My north star is making learning systems that behave well when the world doesn’t cooperate. I like work that connects modeling choices to practical evaluation: distribution shift, spurious correlations, and how to use mechanistic structure when it helps.

Medical imaging & augmentation

Stanford Biomedical Informatics · skin lesions · lung motion simulation

I work on bias/confounder mitigation for image diagnosis and on physiologically plausible data augmentation. Recent work includes simulating lung motion (FEA) to generate breathing-cycle variations that improve tumor segmentation.

Generative + analytical vision

stereoscopic view synthesis

I’ve explored stereoscopic image spatialization via conditional GANs, depth-based analytical methods, and hybrid composites that combine both to improve perceptual quality.

Environmental ML

microplastics · segmentation · generalization

For microplastics identification, we use GAN-generated ecological context to increase training diversity and improve segmentation performance across varied water samples.

ML systems that ship

data pipelines · retrieval · deployment

I like building end-to-end systems: ingestion pipelines, model training, APIs, and monitoring. It’s the fastest way I know to learn what matters in practice.
